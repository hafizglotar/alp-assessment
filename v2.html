The product

Introducing Harbourstone Income Fund I

A UK long-income real estate strategy with a clear social purpose: housing people who need it most, while generating long-term contracted income for investors.

Homes with a purpose

Built around the people who need a stable home the most

Everyone deserves a place they can call home — a safe haven they can rely on long term. Few need that certainty more than people living with enduring illnesses, who depend on living space designed for their specialist needs.

Harbourstone Income Fund I is built around assisted living accommodation for exactly those tenants. Working alongside housing associations and Community Benefit Societies, the fund supports fit-for-purpose homes let on 25-year agreements underpinned by the Department for Work and Pensions (DWP).

The objective is to deliver more than 100 assisted living apartments across the UK — and dependable, long-term income for the investors who help make them possible.
